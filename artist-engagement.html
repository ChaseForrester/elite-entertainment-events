1. Engagement models

Artists may be engaged as independent contractors. Nothing in a booking creates employment unless expressly agreed in writing.

2. Copyright & performances

Live performances of musical works require appropriate APRA AMCOS / PPCA licences at the venue where applicable. Clients are responsible for venue licences unless we expressly arrange them.

3. Moral rights & recording

Recording or livestream of artists requires prior written approval and may attract additional fees. Moral rights under the Copyright Act 1968 (Cth) must be respected.

4. Workplace health & safety

Shared WHS duties apply between venues, PCBU clients and contractors. Risk assessments for staging, pyro, crowd and electrical work must be completed before show call.

5. Discrimination & harassment

We do not tolerate discrimination, sexual harassment or vilification under federal and State equal opportunity laws. Reports can be made to info@eeevents.com.au.

6. Minors

Performances involving minors comply with State child employment and supervision rules. Working with Children Checks are required for relevant roles.
